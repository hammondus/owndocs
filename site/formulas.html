What it is Self-host Formulas Comparison

Formulas

Every function the sheet engine can call. A formula starts with =, references cells as A1 and ranges as A1:B10, and & joins text.

Numbers

SUM(value, …)
Adds numbers, or everything in a range.=SUM(A1:A10)
AVERAGE(value, …)
The mean of the numbers given.=AVERAGE(B1:B12)
MIN(value, …)
The smallest number.=MIN(A1:A10)
MAX(value, …)
The largest number.=MAX(A1:A10)
ROUND(number, places)
Rounds to a number of decimal places.=ROUND(A1, 2)
ABS(number)
Drops the sign.=ABS(-4)
SQRT(number)
Square root.=SQRT(16)
MOD(number, divisor)
The remainder after division.=MOD(7, 3)

Counting

COUNT(value, …)
How many of them are numbers.=COUNT(A1:A10)
COUNTA(value, …)
How many are not empty, whatever they hold.=COUNTA(A1:A10)

Logic

IF(test, then, else)
Picks one of two values. Only the branch taken is worked out, so an error in the other cannot spoil it.=IF(A1>10, "over", "under")
AND(test, …)
True when every one of them is.=AND(A1>0, B1>0)
OR(test, …)
True when any one of them is.=OR(A1>0, B1>0)
NOT(test)
Turns true into false and back.=NOT(A1>0)

Text

CONCATENATE(text, …)
Joins text end to end. & does the same thing.=CONCATENATE(A1, " ", B1)
LEN(text)
How many characters.=LEN(A1)
LEFT(text, count)
The first few characters.=LEFT(A1, 3)
RIGHT(text, count)
The last few characters.=RIGHT(A1, 3)
MID(text, start, count)
Characters from the middle, counting from 1.=MID(A1, 2, 3)
UPPER(text)
Everything in capitals.=UPPER(A1)
LOWER(text)
Everything in lower case.=LOWER(A1)
TRIM(text)
Removes spaces from both ends.=TRIM(A1)

Dates and times

TODAY()
Today's date.=TODAY()
NOW()
The date and time right now.=NOW()
DATE(year, month, day)
Builds a date from its parts.=DATE(2026, 8, 13)
TIME(hour, minute, second)
Builds a time from its parts.=TIME(9, 30, 0)
YEAR(date)
The year out of a date.=YEAR(A1)
MONTH(date)
The month, 1 to 12.=MONTH(A1)
DAY(date)
The day of the month.=DAY(A1)
HOUR(time)
The hour, 0 to 23.=HOUR(A1)
MINUTE(time)
The minutes.=MINUTE(A1)
SECOND(time)
The seconds.=SECOND(A1)
WEEKDAY(date)
Day of the week, 1 for Sunday.=WEEKDAY(A1)
DAYS(end, start)
How many days between two dates.=DAYS(B1, A1)
EDATE(date, months)
The same day, a number of months away.=EDATE(A1, 3)

In Google Sheets, not in Own Docs

Google Sheets listed 515 functions on 2026-10-19, and Own Docs has 35 of them by name. Each of the 35 does the same job as the Google function of that name. Nobody has tested them against Google Sheets, so an edge case can differ.

Google also lists 15 operators as functions. Own Docs has the operators and not the names: ADD is +, MINUS is -, MULTIPLY is *, DIVIDE is /, POW is ^, UMINUS is -x, UPLUS is +x, UNARY_PERCENT is x%, CONCAT is &, EQ is =, NE is <>, GT is >, GTE is >=, LT is <, LTE is <=.

These 10 need the network or a Google service. Own Docs sheets have neither, on purpose: AI DETECTLANGUAGE GOOGLEFINANCE GOOGLETRANSLATE IMAGE IMPORTDATA IMPORTFEED IMPORTHTML IMPORTRANGE IMPORTXML
